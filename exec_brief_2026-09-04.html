  • Effort is running +17% against estimate. 860 work items carry an estimate — a field absent from earlier exports — showing 3,620h actual against 3,101h planned, with 172 items past double. Give it another month before drawing a conclusion.
  • DevOps completion has fallen three months running — 99.0% down to 85.7% (Jun'26 cohort), while the other stream held. The newest cohort has only just passed the 60-day maturity line, so part of the drop may still close out. Re-read in four weeks.
  • Utilisation dropped from 95.5% in Jun'26 to 63.5% in Aug'26 — 2,239 hours across 21 people. No leave calendar exists in the data, so this could be absence, under-logging, or genuine slack. One question to the team leads before it is read as capacity.
  • Three owners hold 55% of open work. Pradeep Singh Rawat 332, Sonu Kumar 262, Shafquat Imam 180, of 1,401 across 28 owners. Much of this is likely dormant projects inflating the count — re-measure after the reconciliation.
  • Bug rate is not a usable signal for DevOps. It swings between 0% and 83% month to month, which points to batch-logging rather than genuine quality movement.
  • DevOps is the weakest group on schedule. 24 of 30 projects past due (80%), against 44% in Power BI.

Confidence in this brief

  • Ticket integrity is the one hard blocker. 2,002 tickets are analysable after excluding 136 dated in the future, 130 of which are already marked Finished. A further 453 carry a due date before their creation date. Exclusions are defensible; the export is not yet a trustworthy system of record.
  • Projects and timesheets join on name alone, matching 40% of the time. The two systems use unrelated ID schemes, so 42 of the 71 past-due projects cannot be checked against effort at all. The 21 dormant and 8 evergreen splits are solid; the rest are unverified.
  • Only 16 project lines received logged time in 90 days against 124 active projects. The naming mismatch above inflates that gap, so treat it as directional rather than exact.
  • The two streams are Project Groups, not Scrum and Kanban. No board-type field exists in the current export, so DevOps and Ticket Mgmt (M&S) are the closest real split the data supports. Read the charts as project delivery versus maintenance.
  • No KPI targets exist anywhere in the source. The thresholds colouring the tiles are proposals set in config.TARGETS pending sign-off, and the dashed chart lines are 12-month medians computed from the data itself — not agreed thresholds.
  • Completion is measured on matured cohorts. Items count only once their creation month is at least 60 days old, because measuring recent months directly makes them look catastrophic purely from immaturity. The chart therefore ends at Jun'26, not Aug'26.
  • Two KPIs rest on thin samples. Test pass rate covers 613 of 15,342 work items (4%), and RCA is populated on 7,164. Both are directionally useful; neither should be trended yet.
  • Utilisation assumes 8 hours per business day with no leave calendar, so it overstates any month containing holiday or absence.
  • All three feeds are current — Zoho Projects 3d · Timesheets 1d · Service Desk 0d — so cross-checks between them are sound.
  • Zoho Print is still absent. No export supplied and no definition of what it should contribute to the brief.
